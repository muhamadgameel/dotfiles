**A WheelHandler never sees ScrollBegin:** `wantsPointerEvent` rejects an event with no delta in its orientation while the handler is not active, so the first version (reset at Begin) carried one swipe's remainder into the next (3 steps, then the first step of the next swipe six events late); a MouseArea is given two Begin and two End events. Hence the reset at End, and hence a vertical handler also lets a sideways-only swipe through.
